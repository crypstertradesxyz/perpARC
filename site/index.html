The keeper

An agent runs your position.

The PerpArc keeper watches every paired wallet on Arc. When fees land, it splits them, bridges the position share to Hyperliquid through Circle's CCTP, and keeps your position at the leverage you picked. No one has to press a button.

How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself.

On Coinbarrel, whoever launches a token decides where its fees go. You decide to send them to a position.

  1. Get your wallet

    Pick a Hyperliquid market, long or short, and a leverage multiple. PerpArc generates a wallet for that position.

  2. Launch with it

    When you launch on Coinbarrel, set that wallet as your revenue controller. That choice is made once, at launch.

  3. The keeper takes over

    As fees arrive, it splits them, funds your position, and holds it at your leverage.

Risk

What can go wrong.

  • Positions get liquidated.

    A liquidated position reopens at the same market, side, and leverage. The lost margin isn't recovered.

  • Your controller choice is hard to undo.

    On Coinbarrel, only its platform admin can change a token's revenue controller after launch. We checked this on-chain.

  • The keeper holds the wallet.

    PerpArc's keeper controls your PerpArc wallet's key. Treat it like any custodial deposit address.

  • Token price isn't position price.

    They're linked only through buybacks. Your token can fall while the position does well, or the reverse.

  • Arc is new.

    Arc mainnet opened in September 2026. Bridges and integrations on it are still young.

  • This isn't investment advice.

    Leverage cuts both ways, for the position and for everyone holding the token.

Redirect your fees. We fund the position.

Launch your token however you like on Arc through Argus. Pair it with ArcPerp and we hand you a wallet address — redirect your claimed fees there, and we turn them into an isolated, leveraged position on Hyperliquid, long or short, at whatever multiple you picked when you paired.

Fee currency: native Arc USDC — no swap needed before it reaches your wallet. From your ArcPerp wallet: capital bridges on to Hyperliquid, the rest sits in its own buyback wallet.

How pairing works

First

Launch on Argus, however you like

ArcPerp doesn't launch your token for you. Use Argus's own launchpad on Arc, set whatever buy/sell tax you want — the token is entirely yours.

Then

Pair it, and get a wallet

Tell us the Hyperliquid asset, side, and leverage multiple you want. We generate a wallet address for that pairing — nothing on your token changes.

Ongoing

Redirect your fees, we do the rest

Point your own claimed Argus fees at that address. Once a balance crosses the funding threshold, we bridge it to Hyperliquid and manage an isolated position for it.

What can go wrong

  • Positions get liquidated. When one does, it reopens at the same asset, side, and multiple — the loss isn't recovered or made up elsewhere.
  • The token's price isn't the position's price. They're linked only through buybacks; a token can fall while its position is fine, or the reverse.
  • You control the redirect. Pointing your fees at your ArcPerp wallet is a choice you make and can change — nothing about your token is locked to us.
  • Arc and Argus are new. Arc mainnet opened this month. Pairing depends on Argus's live contracts working as documented, not a simulation of them.
  • Nothing here is investment advice. Leverage cuts both ways, for the position and for anyone holding the token.

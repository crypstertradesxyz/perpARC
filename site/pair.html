Before you request

  • Your token needs to already be trading on Argus, on Arc — PerpArc doesn't launch it for you.
  • Asset, direction, and leverage are fixed once pairing is confirmed. Choose deliberately.
  • You keep claiming your own Argus fees yourself; pairing only tells you where to redirect them.
  • The address is generated instantly and deterministically from your token's address — requesting again for the same token returns the same address.

Custody: this address is controlled by PerpArc's keeper, not by you. Treat it like any custodial deposit address until your position is live.
